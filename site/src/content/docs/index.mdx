Streamline reads your real watch history from Netflix, Prime Video, Apple TV, Plex, and manual lists.
It looks every title up on TMDB, writes a short description of each with an LLM, and builds a taste profile from the titles you say you loved.
Then you ask for something in plain English, and it answers with picks you haven't seen, explained against your own taste.

It is a single-user app you run yourself, on a laptop or a home server.
The screenshots on this site come from a demo library of famous titles, not anyone's real history.
