# Oppenheimer

Christopher Nolan's methodical yet propulsive drama traces J. Robert Oppenheimer's feverish ascent and moral reckoning with unflinching intensity, interweaving intimate domestic tensions with the crushing weight of scientific ambition and Cold War paranoia. The film pulses with the fractured, non-linear rhythm of a conscience unraveling—oscillating between New Mexico's desert testing grounds and D.C.'s interrogation chambers—as it grapples with the paradox of patriotism twisted into existential dread and the terrible knowledge that genius has unleashed something irrevocable upon humanity. Austere yet intimate, it's a portrait of American exceptionalism corroded from within, where Cillian Murphy's haunted performance captures a man undone not by external enemies but by his own complicity in reshaping the world's capacity for destruction.
