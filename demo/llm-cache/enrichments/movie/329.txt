# Jurassic Park

This breathless adventure pulses with 1990s techno-optimism colliding spectacularly against primal chaos, as a visionary billionaire's audacious DNA-resurrection theme park descends into survival horror when his genetic creations—from graceful brontosaurs to the terrifying T-Rex—break free on an isolated Costa Rican island. Spielberg orchestrates an exhilarating tonal whiplash between wonder and terror, grounding the fantastical science in genuine paleontological reverence while delivering white-knuckle set pieces that prioritize visceral thrills over philosophical hand-wringing. The film's restless momentum, anchored by the ensemble's earnest performances and punctuated by John Williams' soaring score, captures the intoxicating hubris of unchecked ambition and the humbling realization that nature—especially 65-million-year-old nature—refuses to be contained by human arrogance or electric fences.
