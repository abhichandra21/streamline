# Andhadhun

This darkly comedic Indian thriller pulses with unpredictable energy, weaving a Hitchcockian cat-and-mouse game around a blind pianist who witnesses a murder and becomes entangled with a morally ambiguous couple in a claustrophobic web of deception. Sriram Raghavan crafts a razor-sharp narrative that oscillates between tense psychological suspense and pitch-black humor, anchored by Ayushmann Khurrana's nuanced performance as a man whose blindness becomes both liability and strategic advantage. The film's Hindi idiom and jazzy, discordant score underscore themes of perception versus reality, complicity, and how circumstance—not virtue—determines survival in a morally corrupt world.
