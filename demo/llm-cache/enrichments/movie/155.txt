# The Dark Knight

Christopher Nolan's *The Dark Knight* is a relentlessly paced, morally ambiguous crime thriller that strips away superhero mythology to explore the psychological and philosophical toll of vigilantism against systemic corruption. Heath Ledger's sadistic Joker—a force of pure chaos designed to expose Gotham's fragile moral architecture—propels a taut cat-and-mouse narrative that trades quips for interrogations and spectacle for suffocating tension, anchored by Christian Bale's increasingly isolated Batman grappling with whether his methods can ever justify their ends. The film adopts the steely, procedural tone of Michael Mann's crime dramas rather than comic-book conventions, crafting an urban dystopia where every protagonist—criminal, cop, and vigilante alike—confronts the corrupting seduction of absolute power.
