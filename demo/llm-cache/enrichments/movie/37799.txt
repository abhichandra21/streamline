# The Social Network

David Fincher's razor-sharp docudrama pulses with the manic energy of ambition colliding with betrayal, propelled by rapid-fire dialogue and a propulsive score that mirrors the frenetic pace of Silicon Valley's rise. Set against the backdrop of Harvard and Boston's tech scene, the film captures the cold, isolating brilliance of Mark Zuckerberg with clinical precision—contrasting intimate scenes of coding genius with the calculating ruthlessness of venture capitalism and interpersonal destruction. Fincher transforms the founding of Facebook into a modern tragedy about genius, jealousy, and the hollow victory of disruption, where every innovation is shadowed by lawsuits, resentment, and the question of who truly deserves credit for changing the world.
