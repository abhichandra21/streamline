# Knives Out

Rian Johnson crafts a wickedly clever whodunit that luxuriates in the trappings of classic murder mysteries while gleefully subverting genre expectations, anchored by Daniel Craig's deliciously affected Southern-accented detective navigating the Byzantine secrets of a Massachusetts mansion's dysfunctional dynasty. The film moves with snappy, propulsive energy that mirrors a Agatha Christie page-turner, balancing comedic irreverence with genuine detective-work ingenuity as it peels back the layers of a wealthy family's greed and entitlement. Threaded throughout is a quietly potent commentary on class resentment and immigrant vulnerability, crystallized in Ana de Armas's sympathetic portrayal of a working-class caretaker caught between her conscience and survival, lending moral weight beneath the stylish neo-noir surface.
