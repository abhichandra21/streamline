# Spotlight

This methodical procedural traces the Boston Globe's dogged investigation into the Catholic Church's systematic cover-up of clergy sexual abuse with a deliberately paced, almost journalistic restraint that lets meticulous reporting and moral gravity do the heavy lifting rather than sensationalism. The ensemble cast embodies the unglamorous work of verification and persistence, creating a tense but measured examination of institutional corruption that privileges dialogue and documentation over dramatic flourishes. Anchored in 1970s-80s Boston's blue-collar Catholic culture, the film treats journalism itself as a moral act—one that requires patience, collaboration, and the courage to challenge untouchable power structures.
