# Dangal

This exhilarating Hindi-language biographical drama chronicles a Haryana wrestler's fierce transformation of his daughters into national champions, blending the bruising intensity of training montages with moments of humor and genuine tenderness that reveal the complexity beneath his tyrannical coaching methods. Rooted in small-town Indian culture and wrestling tradition, the film navigates the tension between patriarchal control and feminist awakening, as his daughters internalize both his demanding discipline and their own agency, creating a pulsing narrative that celebrates female empowerment while unflinchingly examining the psychological costs of paternal ambition. Aamir Khan's volatile performance anchors the film's oscillating tone—gritty and comedic by turns—ensuring it never devolves into simple inspirational sentiment, instead capturing the messy, contradictory reality of a family bound by sport, sacrifice, and hard-won respect.
