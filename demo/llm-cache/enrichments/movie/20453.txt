# 3 Idiots

This Hindi coming-of-age dramedy zips between past and present with infectious energy, balancing slapstick humor and heartfelt moments as it follows three engineering students navigating India's rigid academic system and middle-class expectations. Director Rajkumar Hirani weaves sharp social commentary about rote learning and parental pressure into the film's rollicking narrative, where friendship becomes the counterweight to institutional conformity. The film's tone—buoyant yet reflective, comedic yet earnest—captures the rebellion and vulnerability of youth while celebrating unconventional thinking in a culture obsessed with credentials and success.
