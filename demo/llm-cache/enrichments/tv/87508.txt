# Delhi Crime

This visceral Indian true crime series unfolds the meticulous police investigation into the 2012 Delhi gang rape with unflinching intensity, balancing procedural rigor with the emotional weight of systemic failure and societal trauma. Director Richie Mehta crafts a deliberate, methodical pace punctuated by moments of horrified realization, while the Hindi dialogue grounds the narrative in Delhi's gritty streets and institutional bureaucracy, capturing both the euphoria of investigative breakthroughs and the tragic scope of the crime. Anchored by Shefali Shah's steely determination as a police officer navigating patriarchal resistance, the series explores themes of institutional negligence, female agency, and the collision between India's modernizing aspirations and its deeply entrenched gender violence.
