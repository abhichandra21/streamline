# Line of Duty

This taut British procedural throttles forward with relentless interrogation scenes and Byzantine conspiracy plots, unraveling systemic police corruption through the eyes of an obsessive anti-corruption unit that operates with bureaucratic precision and moral ambiguity. Jed Mercurio crafts a distinctly UK procedural flavored by institutional paranoia and class-conscious class dynamics, where every interview room confrontation crackles with psychological gamesmanship and verbal sparring rather than violence. The show's icy, methodical tone—punctuated by shocking revelations that reframe previous seasons—treats corruption not as individual moral failure but as an insidious web of institutional loyalty, blackmail, and complicity that corrodes the entire system from within.
