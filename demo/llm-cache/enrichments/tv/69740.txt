# Ozark

This propulsive Midwestern noir follows a meek financial advisor's calculated descent into cartel money laundering, where the humid malaise of rural Missouri becomes a pressure cooker for moral compromise and domestic fracture. The show's deliberate pacing builds suffocating dread through procedural detail and claustrophobic family dynamics, balancing Jason Bateman's understated menace against Laura Linney's fierce protectiveness as they navigate an increasingly treacherous criminal underworld. Rooted in the specificity of Ozark geography and populated by volatile kingpins, duplicitous officials, and a breakout Julia Garner as a feral teenage criminal prodigy, it's a show about how greed metastasizes from ambition into existential entrapment.
