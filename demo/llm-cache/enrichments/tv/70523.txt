# Dark

This German noir thriller plunges into a labyrinthine mystery where a detective's investigation into a child's disappearance in a claustrophobic small town spirals into a mind-bending exploration of time travel and interconnected destinies spanning generations. The pacing deliberately builds from intimate, grounded detective work to cosmic-scale revelations, balancing intimate character drama with high-concept sci-fi while maintaining a brooding, atmospheric tone that reflects the shadowy forests and looming nuclear facility that define the town's landscape. Thematically obsessed with fate, guilt, and the cyclical nature of tragedy, it weaves existential dread throughout its examination of how past sins inevitably shape the future, rooted in distinctly German sensibilities of burden and consequence.
