# Chernobyl

Craig Mazin's meticulous HBO miniseries unfolds the 1986 nuclear disaster with a suffocating, procedural dread that prioritizes historical accuracy and Soviet bureaucratic tragedy over spectacle, anchored by Jared Harris's haunted portrayal of a physicist confronting systemic denial and deception. The pacing deliberately builds tension through bureaucratic obstacles and cover-ups rather than action, creating a bleak, methodical atmosphere that captures both the technical horror of the catastrophe and the political machinery that compounded it. Infused with 1980s Soviet Cold War tension and Ukrainian suffering, the drama examines themes of institutional corruption, individual sacrifice, and the human cost of prioritizing state reputation over truth.
