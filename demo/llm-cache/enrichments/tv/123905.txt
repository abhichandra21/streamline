# Scam

This Russian comedy moves at a breakneck, farcical pace as a ragtag crew of small-time hustlers stumbles through increasingly absurd confidence schemes with more luck than cunning, their bungling escapades capturing the irreverent, street-smart humor of post-Soviet culture. The tone oscillates between slapstick chaos and sharp-tongued banter, with Styopin and Tsaregorodtsev anchoring the ensemble's chemistry as they navigate moral ambiguity through comedy rather than judgment. Thematically, it explores survival and reinvention in a murky economic landscape where con artistry becomes both a cynical critique of social climbing and a darkly comedic meditation on how ordinary people adapt—and occasionally thrive—in systems that reward deception.
